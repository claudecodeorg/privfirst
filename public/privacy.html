PrivFirst — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

PrivFirst is a suite of utilities (PDF tools, image tools, encrypted notes, developer tools, and calculators) that run entirely on your device, in your browser. This policy applies to the PrivFirst web app and to the PrivFirst Android app, which packages the same code as a native app with no server and no internet access of its own (see "App permissions" below).

What we collect

Nothing. PrivFirst has no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no user accounts. There is nothing for us to collect, because your files, text and inputs never leave your device — every tool (PDF editing, image compression, QR scanning, encryption, and the rest) runs locally in your browser or the app's embedded browser view.

What's stored on your device

Some tools save data only in your browser's local storage, on your device, so it's available the next time you open the app:

  • Encrypted Notes: your notes, encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We never see the passphrase or the notes; there is no way for us to recover either.
  • Favourites and recently-used tools: which tools you've starred or opened.
  • Pinned time zones and similar per-tool preferences.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it permanently.

Camera access

The QR code scanner asks for camera access only when you tap "Scan with camera" — scanning from an existing image never requires it. Camera frames are processed locally and are never recorded, stored or transmitted. On Android, the permission is requested at that moment (never at install or launch) and the app gives it up again as soon as you stop scanning.

App permissions (Android app only)

The Android app requests no internet permission and no storage or media permission — it cannot make network requests, read your files, or access photos/media on your device, even in principle. It requests only:

  • Camera — only for the optional live QR scan described above, and only while scanning. The app installs and works fully without ever granting this.
  • A one-time-purchase billing permission — lets the app talk to the Google Play Store app on your device for the single in-app purchase described below. This does not grant network access; it works only with the Play Store app already installed on your device.

Saving a file (a PDF, an image, etc.) uses Android's standard "Save As" file picker, which needs no storage permission.

Purchases (Android app only)

The Android app offers a one-time "lifetime access" purchase after a one-day free trial, handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. There is no server of ours involved in this purchase: your purchase status is checked directly against Google Play each time you open the app, and cached on your device so the app keeps working offline after you've bought it. The free web version at this same address is not affected by this and remains free and unrestricted.

Third parties

PrivFirst does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

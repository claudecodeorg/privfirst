PrivFirst — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

PrivFirst is a suite of utilities (PDF tools, image tools, encrypted notes, developer tools, and calculators) that run entirely on your device, in your browser. This policy applies to the PrivFirst web app and to the PrivFirst Android app, which is the same web app packaged for the Play Store.

What we collect

Nothing. PrivFirst has no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no user accounts. There is nothing for us to collect, because your files, text and inputs never leave your device — every tool (PDF editing, image compression, QR scanning, encryption, and the rest) runs locally in your browser or the app's embedded browser view.

What's stored on your device

Some tools save data only in your browser's local storage, on your device, so it's available the next time you open the app:

  • Encrypted Notes: your notes, encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We never see the passphrase or the notes; there is no way for us to recover either.
  • Favourites and recently-used tools: which tools you've starred or opened.
  • Pinned time zones and similar per-tool preferences.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it permanently.

Camera access

The QR code scanner asks for camera access to scan a code. Camera frames are processed locally and are never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Purchases (Android app only)

The Android app offers a one-time "lifetime access" purchase after a one-day free trial, handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The free web version at this same address is not affected by this and remains free and unrestricted.

Third parties

PrivFirst does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
